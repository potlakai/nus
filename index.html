Questions

FAQ

Is this a cheating tool?

No. It plans, schedules, briefs, and quizzes you. It refuses to write your submissions, and it reads your school's AI policy straight out of the syllabus.

What do I need to run it?

Windows, and your own AI. Claude runs the desktop app: if Claude Code is installed, Nūs uses your subscription; otherwise paste an Anthropic API key. Gemini runs the Companion, and Google offers a free tier through AI Studio. Both keys are encrypted on your device and only ever sent to the provider you chose.

What does the $9.99 actually buy?

Not the model; you pay the provider directly on both plans. Pro buys the layer around it with the caps removed: unlimited imports and questions, the Companion all day with full history, both calendars, unlimited rules, more storage. Free is $0 forever and enough to run a real semester.

I bought Pro on the website. How does the app know?

Pro belongs to your Nūs account, not to a download. Sign in with the same Google account or email inside the app and the Plan card flips to Pro on its own, usually within seconds. Your coursework never leaves your machine; the account only carries who you are and whether you subscribe.

Why does Windows warn me about the download?

The installer is not code signed yet. A certificate costs money and I would rather ship first. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The source is on GitHub if you would rather build it yourself.

Is it really only Windows?

For now. It is built on Electron, so the Mac build is a build target and a round of testing, not a rewrite. It is next on the list; say so here and it moves up.
