Set-up

Running in 10 minutes.

Three steps from download to a semester Nūs understands.

  1. 01Download and open itUse the installer for automatic updates, or the portable build.
  2. 02Give it your AIIf Claude Code is installed, Nūs finds it. Otherwise paste an Anthropic API key. Pointing at your screen uses that same Claude connection; everyday Companion questions use a Gemini key.
  3. 03Drop in a syllabusCheck the table, confirm what it read, and your semester is mapped.
Your syllabi. Your notes. Your recordings. They never reach our server. That is the architecture.

Everything lives in a local database on your machine. Your account holds two things: who you are, and whether you subscribe. The only calls that leave your device go to the AI provider you chose, Supabase for plan status, and Stripe when you upgrade.

Questions

FAQ

Is this a cheating tool?

No. It plans, schedules, briefs, and quizzes you. It refuses to write your submissions, and it reads your school's AI policy straight out of the syllabus.

What do I need to run it?

Windows, and your own AI. Claude runs the desktop app: if Claude Code is installed, Nūs uses your subscription; otherwise paste an Anthropic API key. Gemini runs the Companion's everyday questions, and Google offers a free tier through AI Studio. Pointing at the screen with Ctrl+Shift+T goes to Claude through the same connection the app uses. Both keys are encrypted on your device and only ever sent to the provider you chose.

What does the $9.99 actually buy?

Not the model; you pay the provider directly on both plans. Pro buys the layer around it with the caps removed: unlimited imports and questions, the Companion all day with full history, both calendars, unlimited rules, more storage. Free is $0 forever and enough to run a real semester.

I bought Pro on the website. How does the app know?

Pro belongs to your Nūs account, not to a download. Sign in with the same Google account or email inside the app and the Plan card flips to Pro on its own, usually within seconds. Your coursework never leaves your machine; the account only carries who you are and whether you subscribe.

Why does Windows warn me about the download?

The installer is not code signed yet. A certificate costs money and I would rather ship first. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The source is on GitHub if you would rather build it yourself.

Is it really only Windows?

For now. It is built on Electron, so the Mac build is a build target and a round of testing, not a rewrite. It is next on the list; say so here and it moves up.
