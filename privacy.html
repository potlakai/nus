The fine print

Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026 · applies to the Nūs app and this website (potlakai.github.io/nus)

Nūs was built around one idea: your coursework should stay on your device. The desktop app uses a local database and managed local source files. Optional Nūs accounts use Supabase for identity and subscription status, but Nūs does not upload your syllabi, tasks, Companion transcripts, or chat history to that account.

1. The Nūs app

Your semester content stays local. Courses, deadlines, tasks, imports, chats, and Companion transcripts live in the Nūs data folder on your computer. Nūs accounts store an email address, user identifier, and subscription status in Supabase. Stripe processes payment details when you choose to buy Pro; Nūs does not receive or store your full card number. Local crash logs may record technical diagnostics such as the app version and error stack, but are designed not to include imported content.

Account identity and subscription records are kept while your account is active and as needed to operate or document the subscription. You can ask to have your Nūs account data deleted using the contact address below. Stripe may retain payment and transaction records under its own legal obligations and privacy terms.

One thing to understand: Nūs uses the AI provider you configure. When you ask Nūs to read a syllabus or answer a question, the relevant content is processed by that provider under your account and its privacy terms. Nūs currently supports Claude for the desktop assistant and Gemini or another configured provider for the Companion. Nūs does not proxy those prompts through its own model server.

Downloading the installer from GitHub Releases is subject to GitHub's standard download logs, same as any file hosted there. The installer checks GitHub Releases for updates when you run it; that request carries the app version and nothing about your content.

2. This website

The site is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses:

  • GoatCounter for basic, privacy-friendly analytics: page views, referrers, browser type, and country, in aggregate. No cookies, no advertising identifiers, no cross-site tracking, and nothing that builds a profile of you. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages for hosting. GitHub may log standard technical data (like IP addresses) to serve and secure the site, per the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts to load the site's typeface; your browser requests the font files from Google's servers (Google's privacy policy).

3. Accounts, Pro, and the idea form

Signing in on this site (on the Account page) uses the same Supabase project as the app. Supabase receives your email address and, if you use Google, the identity Google returns; it stores your email, a user identifier, and your plan. Buying Pro sends you to Stripe Checkout on Stripe's own pages (Stripe's privacy policy); the card never passes through this site or the app. Your browser keeps your Nūs session in local storage until you sign out.

The idea box on the home page is saved in your own browser and copied to your clipboard; nothing is sent anywhere until you paste it into the public GitHub discussion thread yourself.

4. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU/UK GDPR and the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data we hold about you, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. This may include your Nūs account email, user identifier, subscription status, and information you submit through a form. Email us and we will respond to a verified request as required by applicable law. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

5. Children

Nūs, this site, and its forms are not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child submitted personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Changes

If Nūs or its services change, this policy will be updated here with a new effective date.

7. Contact
